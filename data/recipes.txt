[NAME]: Americano 30ml 
[INGREDIENTS]: Coffee beans, Water 
[METHOD]: Espresso machine at 92°C. Aerate water using machine's steam to around 65°C, then mix espresso into water. Remove crema. 
[BREW_TIME]: 3 minutes 
[RATING]: 3.5/5 
[NOTES]: Grind size: 7 on Timemore C3. Coffee dose: 15g. Works better with light to medium roast beans.
